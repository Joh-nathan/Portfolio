Beads and Belief: A Prayer in Motion

As the clock struck 4:00 p.m. on October 22, 2025, the school's quadrangle slowly transformed into a sea of white. Hundreds of students, teachers, and staff covered in white t-shirts stood together under the warm embrace of the afternoon sun for the annual Living Rosary. The simple color carried an insightful message: a symbol of commitment to justice and compassion, virtues needed in these challenging times of corruption and calamity. The event began with a solemn procession around the school campus, with participants carrying flowers and images of Our Lady of the Pillar with steady and respectful hands, filling the campus with an atmosphere of sincerity, peace, and devotion. The atmosphere prepared everyone’s heart for what was to come—the start of the annual Living Rosary. When the procession returned to the quadrangle, the participants, still in a line, waited to be called for their turn before going to the center of the quadrangle to form the rosary. A student and their parent represented a “bead,” holding a vigil candle that they would light, a gentle flame in the afternoon breeze. The sight was stunning, flickering lights illuminating different faces filled with faith, peace, and devotion—a living image of a prayer in motion.

February 14: The day that keeps on giving

February 14: a date on the calendar that signifies Valentine's. A monthly event where those in love come out and celebrate their affection. This is a love shaped by presence, connection, and care with the goal of creating simple acts that carry a lasting impact. PCZC secures CEAP regional spot

Pilar College of Zamboanga City, Inc. (PCZC) claimed victory against Ateneo de Zamboanga University (AdZU) in Sepak Takraw of the Catholic Educational Association of the Philippines (CEAP) Games. The match was held on December 13, 2025, at AdZU’s main campus Multi-Purpose Covered Courts 2. PCZC won the first match, 2-0 (15-12, 15-8), and the second match, 2-1 (15-8, 14-17, 15-13). The win earned PCZC a spot in the CEAP Regionals, allowing the school and team to represent Zamboanga City.
